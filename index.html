Help & knowledge base

Frequently asked questions about AI visibility

Learn the basics of measuring and improving your brand's presence across AI answers.

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is how often and how favourably your brand appears in answers generated by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. It complements traditional search rankings by measuring presence inside AI-generated answers.

How do I check if ChatGPT mentions my brand?

Manual prompts can be a starting point, but results vary by wording and time. LLM Audit helps teams track target prompts, classify mentions and citations, and follow changes over time.

Why isn't my brand showing up in AI answers?

Possible causes include crawlability issues, unclear or incomplete content, missing structured data, and competitors being cited more often. A site audit can help prioritise what to investigate.

How do I improve brand visibility in AI search?

Make important pages crawlable, answer real customer questions clearly, use appropriate structured data, build credible third-party corroboration, and measure changes consistently.

How is AI visibility different from SEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in search result pages. AI visibility focuses on whether your brand is included in a synthesised answer. Both can work together as part of a broader discovery strategy.

Which AI platforms does LLM Audit track?

LLM Audit describes tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, along with AI crawler and referral analytics.
